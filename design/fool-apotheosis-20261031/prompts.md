## 人性归位

Create ONE independent refined anime-painting background illustration, target 2560x1440, wide 16:9. Internal concept name 人性归位; no words in the artwork. This is a NEW composition for Lord of the Mysteries, the instant Klein becomes Sequence Zero The Fool, intended as the final scene in a desktop theme series. It must feel like a god has been born, with overwhelming quiet authority. Do not depict an ordinary wizard portrait.

Actual scene motif: a dark translucent cloak assembled from gray fog and black fluid, completely hollow beneath, with a spectral mask becoming Klein's human face. Creative staging, not an official screenshot. Make the moment monumental, direct and emotionally gripping.

Composition: dramatic slightly low camera, FRONT-FACING entity upright in the RIGHT third, floating above a deep bank of fog, at x79%. His head begins around y18%, body silhouette to y92%; not a close-up headshot. A single long mantle has a high, deep hood framing ONE AND ONLY ONE face. The face is a translucent ivory-silver mask with Klein's narrow mature features, dark hair barely emerging beneath the hood, brown-black eyes open and looking directly outward and slightly down at the viewer; expression grave, utterly composed, resolute, faintly sorrowful, powerful without smiling or rage. Mask's lower left cheek edge is slightly unfinished, dissolving into gray wisps. Not a separate mask floating beside him; no duplicated heads or faces. The inside of the cloak below the neck is a deep void WITHOUT chest, shirt, hands, legs or a physical body. This void must be visually obvious: mantle parts slightly at center to reveal empty darkness, luminous fog passing behind its torn translucent edge. The mantle is an immense heavy shape spreading UP and BACK very subtly like space itself is being folded; a few tiny intricate organic silvery filaments bind it together, with restrained antique-gold threads.

Behind his head and RIGHT shoulder, a narrow break in the gray atmospheric ceiling pours a soft vertical column of cold silver-white light. The cloud layer below is bent into a single broad concave sweep centered beneath him, so he seems to command an immense depth of space. Use a very dim, extremely distant suggestion of one source-castle arch at the extreme right edge for scale, without full buildings, furniture or architecture in the text area. Do not add halo circles, crowns, floating cards, clocks, wings, battle effects or enemies. Godlike feeling comes from the low viewpoint, concentrated frontal gaze, hollow impossible anatomy, vertical light and the submission of the mist. Not from piling symbols.

LEFT 60% strictly low-detail smooth dark blue-charcoal / graphite mist gradient, darkest left edge, slight muted silver haze closer to right, blank and calm enough to place dense light-colored UI text over it. The ONLY bright contrast and fine detail is on the RIGHT 35%. Do not scatter stars, particles, threads, rays or objects across the left.

Style: sophisticated cinematic 2D anime illustration, elegant fine linework, rich painterly brushwork, soft air and tactile diaphanous fabric, deep grayscale with a very subtle blue-plum undertone and tiny old gold highlights, cold sacred mystery and finality. Reference existing sequence-0 ONLY for dark-silver palette and negative space, not the rejected throne pose. Reference sequence-8 ONLY for painterly finish and identity continuity, not clown makeup, clothes, flower, palette or smile. Subject is fully divine yet a trace of Klein's humanity remains in the eyes. No seated figure, no chin-on-hand, no ordinary suit, top hat or monocle, no second person, no grotesque gore. No text, UI, logo, watermark, collage or decorative border.
